Family Chores
by Vista Bahia

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

Vista Bahia does not collect, see, sell or share any of your family's information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your family's chores, photos and allowance live in your own iCloud account, shared only with the people a parent invites.

Where your information is stored

Family Chores stores everything on your device and in your Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. When a parent invites family members, that information is shared with them through iCloud sharing. Vista Bahia runs no servers of its own and has no access to your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

This includes:

  • Family members: names, roles (parent or kid), a chosen emoji and color, and optionally a photo, phone number or email used to send an invite.
  • Chores: titles, notes, schedules, due times, reminder settings and who each chore is assigned to.
  • Completions: when a chore was done, approved or sent back, and any proof photo taken for it.
  • Allowance: weekly totals, locked weeks and payouts a parent records.
  • Badges: which badges each person has earned, the one they're working toward, and any badges kids make from their own photos.
  • Settings: quiet hours, school hours and reminder defaults.
  • Your iCloud account identifier: an anonymous ID from Apple that ties each phone to its family member, so one person can't act as another.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: chore reminders are scheduled on your phone. They aren't sent through any Vista Bahia server.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you take or pick a photo as proof that a chore is done, for your profile picture, or to make a badge. Photos are stored in your family's iCloud.
  • Contacts: when a parent adds someone from Contacts, iOS shares only the one card they pick. The app keeps just that person's first name, photo and the phone number or email used for the invite. It never reads your address book.
  • Calendar (optional): if a parent turns it on, the app adds chores to a "Chores" calendar in your iCloud. It doesn't read your other calendars.
  • Messages and Mail: invites open Messages or Mail with the link filled in. You choose whether to send it.

Children

Family Chores is used by families, including children. Parents set up the family, add each child and control what is shared. Vista Bahia does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app has no advertising, purchases or links to outside websites for kids to follow.

Keeping or deleting your information

A parent can remove a family member, archive or delete chores, and stop sharing with anyone at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from that phone. To remove the family's data from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone. Because Vista Bahia never holds your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts handling information differently, the policy will be updated before that change ships.
