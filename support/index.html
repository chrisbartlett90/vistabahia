Common questions

How do I add my family?

The parent who sets up the family adds everyone in Settings › Family › Add person. Tap the contact button to fill in their name, photo and phone number from Contacts, then tap Add & invite. They'll get a link in Messages. When they open it, their phone opens as them.

The invite says it can't find an Apple Account

Invites go through iCloud, so use the phone number or email that person signs in to iCloud with. You can change it under Settings › Family › their name.

Someone's phone opened as the wrong person

Each person is tied to one iCloud account. A parent can open them under Settings › Family and tap Unlink iCloud account, then that phone can pick the right person.

Why are some reminders silent?

The first reminder is a quiet banner. The second one plays an alarm sound. During school hours, kids' reminders are always silent, and parents hear about missed chores after school. During quiet hours, reminders wait until morning. Change these in Settings › School hours and Quiet hours.

Changes aren't showing on another phone

Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, then open the app. Changes usually arrive within a few seconds to a minute.
